Selected workp. 03
1.

combilive

The problem

Most buses in Lima have no tracking, no journey planner and nothing to press when something goes wrong.

What I built

A real‑time tracking app that needs no extra hardware on the bus. Drivers share their live location from a phone, passengers plan journeys with traffic‑aware arrival times, and operators watch the fleet and emergency alerts as they happen.

Why me

I grew up taking these buses. Friends and family, especially women, have stories of harassment and robbery with nowhere to turn. It started as my dissertation and is now being tested on the streets of Lima.

Result
  • £5,000Cal Henderson Innovation Seed Fund, one of three winners across all computing courses
  • 1stComputing final year project poster, Innovation Festival 2026
