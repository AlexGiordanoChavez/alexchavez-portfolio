## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary: small business owners.** Someone running a business with a messy process (spreadsheets, manual tracking, paperwork) or an app idea, who lands on alexchavez.space from a LinkedIn post, a shared link or a search and needs to decide whether Alex can be trusted with a paid project. They are usually not technical. They judge by: has he built real things, does he explain them plainly, and how do I get in touch.

Secondary visitors also arrive (employers and recruiters, and the BCU / KTP network), but the site is shaped for the small business owner first.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that works as a side channel for paid freelance projects while Alex works full-time as a KTP Associate. It shows real work, real experience and real recognition, and makes contacting him effortless. Success means a small business owner understands what he does, believes he can do it, and sends an email.

## Product Principles

1. **Real over impressive.** Every claim, number and quote is real and traceable; when there is no evidence, say less rather than invent.
2. **Plain words for non-technical owners.** Explain what a project did for people before naming the tools behind it.
3. **Contact is never more than one click away.**
4. **Personal, not corporate.** Alex's voice and story carry the page; it should never read like an agency.
5. **Respect the day job.** Nothing on the site may expose or overstate the confidential KTP work.
