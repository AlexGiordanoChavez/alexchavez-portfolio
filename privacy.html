privacy

The short version

This site has no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no forms. I only receive information about you if you choose to email me.

Who I am

Alex Chavez Gamarra, based in the West Midlands, UK. I run this site and I'm responsible for any information you send me. Contact: hello@alexchavez.space.

When you visit

The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver and protect the site. Fonts, photos and videos are all served from this site itself, so no other company is contacted when you browse it.

When you email me

I receive your email address, your name and whatever you write. I use it only to reply and, if we decide to work together, to plan and deliver the project. I don't sell it, share it or add you to any mailing list.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to work are deleted within 12 months. If we work together, I keep project emails and records for as long as the project needs them and for as long as UK law requires for business records.

Links elsewhere

Links to LinkedIn and YouTube take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies. Nothing from them loads on this site until you click.

Your rights

You can ask what I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to how I use it. Just email me. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
